Design strategy

Time is represented by a calm, trustworthy horizon. Soft sea color describes the shell while one clear action anchors the screen. Best fit for a quiet everyday companion.

Wayfinding typography, direct calls to action and a precise time route. Broadly familiar, efficient and easy to learn. Best fit for first-time users.

Strong typographic numerals and deadline rhythm with no mechanical animation. Best fit for quick reading at high information density.

03 / UNDER THE HOODNO HIDDEN MAGIC

A small app.
Clear intentions.

Purpose-built with Rust and Tauri, Caffeinate controls macOS's own /usr/bin/caffeinate process. Your Mac stays awake for the session you choose, not forever.

NATIVE macOS COMMAND/usr/bin/caffeinate -i -w <app-pid>
⌘

System. Display. Both.

Prevent idle system sleep, display sleep, or both. You always know which mode is active.

◷

Presets and custom timers.

Until stopped, 30 minutes, 1, 2, or 4 hours, or any custom duration from 1 to 1440 minutes.

▤

Profiles and one-time schedules.

Keep useful configurations close. Scheduled actions run only while Caffeinate itself is running.

✳

Built to respect your Mac.

Local history, optional notifications and login launch, light and dark appearance. No cloud connection or telemetry.

05 / THE DETAILSGOOD TO KNOW

A few practical
answers.

Nothing hidden behind an account, plan or complicated setup.

Does Caffeinate keep my Mac awake when I close the window?+

In 3.0, closing the window hides it in the menu bar while an active session continues. Choosing Quit releases the app-owned sleep assertion.

Can it prevent sleep when I close the laptop lid?+

No. Caffeinate controls idle system and display sleep only. It cannot override normal MacBook lid-close behavior.

Do schedules run after I quit the app?+

No. They are one-time, local actions checked while the app is running. They cannot wake a sleeping Mac or continue after Quit.

Does it send data to a server?+

No account, cloud sync, tracking or telemetry is required. The application manages the built-in macOS utility locally.

Is Caffeinate 3.0 available as a public release?+

Not yet. This site previews the local 3.0 beta. The published release and Homebrew installation remain separate until 3.0 has passed its release checks.
